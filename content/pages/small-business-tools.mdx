### First, What Are Small Business Tools?



A business tool is an application or software designed to help organizations run their operations efficiently and effectively. These tools not only allow businesses to stay connected with their employees and customers, but they also help them stay competitive in the marketplace.

With trends and markets developing and changing rapidly in today’s digital world, the right small business tools can help your team stay up\-to\-date and in control.


### The Best Tools for Small Businesses



When time and money are limited, you need to make the most of what you have. The right apps and free small business tools can help you maximize your time without spending a fortune.

So what are the best tools for small business owners? We asked a handful of entrepreneurs to give us some of their top picks in various categories, including marketing, human resources, project management, and more.

### Must\-Have Marketing Tools



To grow your business, customers need to know who you are and how you can help them solve their problems. That’s the core of marketing.

Of course, with the rise of content marketing, the number of media channels you can use to communicate that message has skyrocketed. Between email newsletters and social media posts, it can be difficult to keep track of what you’re trying to say and who you’re trying to talk to.

That is, it’s difficult unless you have an arsenal of business tools at your disposal.

Marketing tools can automate the content publishing process and help you keep all of your channels in sync. Some also provide insights into the performance of your campaigns, so you can tailor your strategy to your target audience.

Best of all, you don’t have to be a marketing expert to use these helpful small business tools. Content marketing is a key driver of ROI, which means there are now a wealth of beginner\-friendly marketing tools to help you stay on track.


### Eye\-Opening Analytics Tools



Of course, you won’t be able to determine whether or not your marketing efforts are working without measuring the results. Analytics tools can help you do just that across all of your marketing channels.

They can collect, organize, and display your data so you can spend more time making informed, strategic decisions. Without this type of analytical support, it can be nearly impossible to find and analyze all of the metrics that help you evaluate the effectiveness of your digital content.

### What Tools Should You Use for Your Small Business?



While these recommendations are a good starting point, only you can determine which small business tools are right for your specific organization. You probably have a list of tasks in your business that you’d like to solve or simplify, whether it’s payroll management or project assignment.

Part of growing a business is discovering new challenges and finding solutions along the way.

So how can you decide which tool is right to solve your top current problems and support your progress in the future?

You can follow the steps we suggest in our HR Software Buyer’s Guide below:

Determine current challenges and what activities would solve those challenges.

Determine the challenges you’re likely to face in the next five years and what activities would solve those challenges.

Identify all the stakeholders who need to weigh in on this decision.

Determine their top concerns for a potential solution.

Research potential small business tools and compile a list.

Identify what features would best support the activities you’ve identified that would solve your challenges.

Identify which features would address each stakeholder’s concerns.

Determine whether each potential tool has the features needed to solve your challenges and address stakeholder concerns.

You may also find a tool of your own that supports your organization in exactly the way you need. Either way, with the right tools and strategy, you can grow your business, even with a shoestring budget and limited time resources.
