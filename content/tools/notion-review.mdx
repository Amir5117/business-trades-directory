  review_title: Notion Pricing Plans
  review_content: 'Notion uses a <strong>per-member subscription model</strong> with monthly and annual
    billing (annual saves about 20%). The Free plan is generous enough for most individuals.


    <strong>Free Plan:</strong> $0/member/mo — unlimited blocks for individuals, up to 10 guest collaborators,
    a trial of Notion AI, basic forms and sites, Notion Calendar, and Notion Mail.

    <strong>Plus Plan:</strong> $10/member/mo billed annually (or $12/member/mo monthly) — unlimited blocks
    for teams, unlimited file uploads, custom forms and sites, unlimited charts, and basic integrations.

    <strong>Business Plan:</strong> $20/member/mo billed annually (or $24/member/mo monthly) — includes
    Notion AI, SAML SSO, private teamspaces, advanced analytics, granular permissions, and premium integrations.

    <strong>Enterprise Plan:</strong> Custom pricing — everything in Business plus zero data retention
    with LLM providers, SCIM user provisioning, advanced security and controls, audit log, and a dedicated
    success manager.


    <strong>Notion AI:</strong> now included in the Business and Enterprise plans. For Free and Plus,
    it is available as a paid add-on.
