    <p>pCloud is a Swiss-based cloud storage service launched in 2013 that has become one of the most
    trusted alternatives to Dropbox, Google Drive, and OneDrive. With over 14 million users worldwide,
    it stands out for its rare lifetime subscription plans, strong privacy protections backed by Swiss
    data laws, and zero-knowledge client-side encryption through pCloud Crypto. The service supports Windows,
    macOS, Linux, iOS, and Android, and includes a virtual disk (pCloud Drive) that lets you treat cloud
    storage like a local hard drive without filling up your physical disk.</p><p>Beyond raw storage, pCloud
    bundles a built-in media player, automatic photo organization, branded file-sharing links, password
    and expiration controls, 30-day file versioning (extendable up to a year), and integrations with social
    media for direct backups. While it lacks an in-browser document editor like Google Docs, its combination
    of speed, security, and one-time payment options has made it a favorite for individuals, photographers,
    and small teams who want to escape recurring subscription fees.</p>'

  review_content: '<h2>pCloud Price Plans</h2>

    <p>pCloud is unique among major cloud storage providers for offering true lifetime plans alongside
    the usual annual subscriptions. The headline benefit: pay once, own your storage forever. Below is
    the current public pricing as of 2026 — note that lifetime tiers are frequently discounted by 50–70%
    during seasonal promotions, so the effective price is often lower than the list price.</p>

    <h3>Free Plan — $0</h3>

    <p>10 GB of free cloud storage with full access to pCloud Drive, the mobile apps, file sharing, and
    the built-in media player. No credit card required, and the 10 GB grows with referrals up to 20 GB
    total.</p>

    <h3>Premium 500 GB — $49.99/year or $199 lifetime</h3>

    <p>500 GB of storage, 500 GB of shared link traffic per month, and full file versioning. The lifetime
    price pays for itself in roughly four years compared to the annual plan.</p>

    <h3>Premium Plus 2 TB — $99.99/year or $399 lifetime</h3>

    <p>2 TB of storage, 2 TB of monthly shared link traffic, and 30-day extended history. This is the
    most popular tier for power users, photographers, and small teams.</p>

    <h3>Ultra 10 TB — $1,190 lifetime (promotional)</h3>

    <p>10 TB of storage with the largest monthly shared link allowance. Aimed at heavy users, content
    creators, and small businesses that need massive long-term storage without recurring fees.</p>

    <h3>pCloud Family — $595 lifetime</h3>

    <p>Shared 2 TB of lifetime storage divided among up to 5 family members, each with their own private
    account.</p>

    <h3>pCloud Business — $9.99/user/month</h3>

    <p>1 TB per user (expandable to 2 TB), team folders, granular permissions, audit logs, account recovery,
    and a dedicated admin panel. Billed per user, with annual discounts available.</p>

    <h3>pCloud Crypto — $4.99/month or $125 lifetime</h3>

    <p>Optional zero-knowledge encryption add-on that creates a private vault encrypted on your device
    before upload. Available as an add-on to any paid plan.</p>
