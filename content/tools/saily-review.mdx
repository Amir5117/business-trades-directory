Saily is an eSIM service by Nord Security (makers of NordVPN) that offers affordable global mobile data plans for travelers across 190\+ countries. In this Saily review by [TheBusinessTrades](https://thebusinesstrades.com/).com, we look at its pricing, coverage, features, and ease of use based on public App Store, Google Play, Trustpilot, and traveler reviews. Whether you are a frequent business traveler, digital nomad, or vacationer, Saily lets you skip roaming fees and stay connected with instant eSIM activation.

Our analysis of Saily travel eSIM reviews shows the platform is trusted for fair pricing, easy setup, and broad coverage. It holds a 4\.6/5 rating on the App Store across 8,500\+ reviews. Users praise the instant activation, no contracts, ad blocker feature, and Nord Security backing, while some flag occasional speed throttling on lower plans. For travelers seeking reliable, cheap mobile data, Saily is a strong choice.
