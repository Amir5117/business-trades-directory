    <p>Clockify pricing is among the most affordable in the time-tracking market. There is a permanently

    than monthly. Below are the current public prices as of 2026.</p>

    <h3>Free Plan — $0</h3>

    <p>Time tracking, project organization, calendar, basic reports, mobile and desktop apps, and browser
    extensions. Free plan now caps at 5 users (changed in April 2026), but remains a great option for
    solo freelancers and very small teams.</p>

    <h3>Basic — $3.99/user/month (annual)</h3>

    <p>Adds bulk edit of time entries, time audit, hide time/pages, project templates, and basic admin
    controls. Great fit for small businesses moving from the free plan.</p>

    <h3>Standard — $5.49/user/month (annual)</h3>

    <p>Adds timesheet approvals, billable hours and invoicing, project budgets, alerts, time-off management,
    and detailed audit logs.</p>

    <h3>Pro — $7.99/user/month (annual)</h3>

    <p>Adds expense tracking, GPS tracking on mobile, optional screenshots, scheduling, capacity planning,
    custom fields, and the Auto Tracker.</p>

    <h3>Enterprise — $11.99/user/month (annual)</h3>

    <p>Adds single sign-on (SSO), custom subdomain, audit logs export, control over user account settings,
    and self-hosted deployment options.</p>
