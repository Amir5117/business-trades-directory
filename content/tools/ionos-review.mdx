Ionos is a major European web hosting and cloud provider offering domains, shared hosting, VPS, dedicated servers, email, and an AI website builder. In this Ionos review by [TheBusinessTrades](https://thebusinesstrades.com/).com, we look at its pricing, performance, features, and customer support based on public Trustpilot, G2, and Hostadvice reviews. Whether you are launching a website, scaling a business, or migrating from a legacy host, Ionos promises enterprise reliability with personal consultants on every plan.

Our analysis of Ionos web hosting reviews shows the platform is trusted for cheap entry\-level plans, European data residency, and dedicated personal consultants. It holds a 4\.5/5 rating on Trustpilot across 52,000\+ reviews. Users praise the low introductory prices, reliable uptime, GDPR compliance, and broad product range, while some flag price increases at renewal. For European businesses and budget\-conscious owners, Ionos remains a strong hosting partner.
