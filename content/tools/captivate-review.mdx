    <span style="font-weight: 400;">When choosing a podcast host, the primary question is whether Captivate.fm

    <h3>1. Captivate vs Buzzsprout</h3>

    <span style="font-weight: 400;">While Buzzsprout is often praised for its simplicity, Captivate is
    the clear winner for creators looking to scale. Unlike Buzzsprout, which charges per episode or hour,
    Captivate allows you to host unlimited podcast shows under a single subscription. This makes it the
    most cost-effective solution for podcast networks and agencies.</span>

    <h3>2. Captivate vs Podbean</h3>

    <span style="font-weight: 400;">Podbean offers a strong mobile presence, but Captivate leads in marketing
    and distribution tools. Features like the Captivate Attribution Links and the highly customizable
    Call-to-Action (CTA) players give it an edge for podcasters focused on lead generation and listener
    conversion.</span>

    <h3>3. Captivate vs Libsyn</h3>

    <span style="font-weight: 400;">Libsyn is a legacy platform, but Captivate offers a much more modern
    and intuitive user interface (UI). Captivate’s built-in SEO-optimized podcast websites outperform
    Libsyn’s basic pages, ensuring your show ranks higher in Google Search and provides a better user
    experience for desktop and mobile listeners.</span>
