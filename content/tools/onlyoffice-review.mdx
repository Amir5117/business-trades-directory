  review_content: '<h2>OnlyOffice Price Plans</h2>

    <p>OnlyOffice has a complex pricing model because it spans free desktop apps, cloud SaaS, collaborative
    DocSpace, full Workspace, and self-hosted enterprise editions. Below are the typical 2026 public prices
    for the most-asked-about tiers.</p>

    <h3>Free Desktop & Mobile Editors — $0</h3>

    <p>Native desktop apps for Windows, macOS, and Linux, plus mobile apps for iOS and Android. Full document,
    spreadsheet, presentation, and PDF editing — completely free for personal use, forever.</p>

    <h3>ONLYOFFICE DocSpace Cloud — Free Startup or $20/admin/month Business</h3>

    <p>Free Startup tier with unlimited users and guests for small teams. Business tier charges only for
    admins at $20/admin/month — a unique pricing model where collaborators are free.</p>

    <h3>ONLYOFFICE Docs Cloud (Business) — $8/user/month</h3>

    <p>Cloud-hosted collaborative document editing with real-time co-editing, version history, and integration
    with most major file storage platforms.</p>

    <h3>ONLYOFFICE Workspace Cloud — From $5/user/month</h3>

    <p>Full collaboration suite with editors, mail, CRM, projects, calendar, and document management.
    Note: Workspace Cloud is being phased out in favour of self-hosted and DocSpace.</p>

    <h3>ONLYOFFICE Docs Self-Hosted — From $1,500 (50 connections, lifetime)</h3>

    <p>Commercial self-hosted licence with one year of updates and support. Scales by number of concurrent
    connections; perpetual licence so you own the software.</p>

    <h3>ONLYOFFICE DocSpace Self-Hosted — From $6,550 lifetime</h3>

    <p>Self-hosted DocSpace with one year of support and updates included. Best for enterprises that need
    full data control and on-premises deployment.</p>

    <li>✅ Built-in audio and video calls inside the editor</li>
