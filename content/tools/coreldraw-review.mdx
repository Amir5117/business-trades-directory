    perpetual license. A 15-day free trial is available (no credit card required). Subscription includes
    automatic upgrades, cloud collaboration, and AI features; perpetual license locks you to one version.


    Monthly Subscription: $39.00/month (single user) - full Suite (CorelDRAW, Photo-Paint, Capture, Font
    Manager), automatic upgrades, cloud storage, AI tools.

    Annual Subscription: $269.00/year (single user) - same features as monthly with annual savings of
    about 40%, ideal for long-term users.

    One-Time Perpetual License (CorelDRAW Graphics Suite 2024/2025): $549.00 (one-time) - indefinite use
    of the purchased version, no upgrades included.

    Maintenance Plan: ~$199/year added on top of perpetual - keeps the perpetual license current with
    the latest major versions, AI credits and premium content.

    Business / Volume Licenses: Custom pricing - tiered pricing for organizations, including CorelSure
    Maintenance and centralized deployment.

    Education Edition: ~$99 perpetual (verified students/teachers) - full-feature non-commercial license.
