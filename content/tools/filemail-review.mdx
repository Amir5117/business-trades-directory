Filemail is a high\-speed file transfer platform that lets you send files of any size, up to 5TB per transfer, without compression, signup, or installation. In this Filemail review by [TheBusinessTrades](https://thebusinesstrades.com/).com, we look at its pricing, features, transfer speeds, and ease of use based on public G2, Trustpilot, and Capterra reviews. Whether you are a video editor, designer, legal professional, or anyone moving large files, Filemail promises ultra\-fast transfers with no file size limits and enterprise\-grade security.

Our analysis of Filemail file transfer reviews shows the platform is trusted for raw speed, no signup requirement for senders, custom branding, and detailed tracking. It holds a 4\.6/5 rating on G2 with 250\+ reviews and high marks on Trustpilot. Users praise the UDP\-accelerated transfers, password protection, white\-label branding, and Outlook integration, while some flag the free tier upload limits. For teams sharing massive files, Filemail is a robust option.
