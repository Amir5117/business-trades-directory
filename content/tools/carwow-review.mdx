    Buyer Pricing: Free - browse offers from manufacturer-approved dealers, compare quotes side-by-side,
    secure delivery anywhere in the UK at no extra cost. The price shown by a dealer is the price the
    buyer pays.

    Private Seller (Sell My Car): Free - upload car details and photos, receive bids from carwow-approved
    dealers and traders, no listing fees, no commission on the sale. Carwow handles paperwork and collection.

    Dealer Lead Fee (New Car): Around GBP 49+VAT per enquiry/sale - non-LMS pricing for dealers receiving
    new car leads.

    Dealer Buyers Fee (Used Car Auctions): From GBP 175 per vehicle - dealers buying used stock through
    carwow Auctions pay a buyer''s fee on top of the hammer price, on average ~10% cheaper than rival
    auction platforms.

    Fleet & Business Buyer: Free - same flow as personal buyers; business users can claim VAT and arrange
    business finance through partnered dealers.

    Finance and Insurance: Optional add-ons priced by partner providers (e.g., HP, PCP, lease deals start
