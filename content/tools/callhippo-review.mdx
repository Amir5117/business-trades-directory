    <p>CallHippo offers four user-per-month tiers plus add-ons, with a 10-day free trial (no credit card
    needed). Prices below are from callhippo.com/pricing (annual billing, USD per user per month for the

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Basic Free</h3>

    <p>$0/user/month. Includes 1 free phone number, voicemail, click to dial, business hours, SMS/MMS,
    WhatsApp Business API, 2FA, and desktop &amp; mobile apps. Note: the free Basic plan is free for the
    first 6 months, after which you upgrade to Starter to continue.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Starter $19/user/month</h3>

    <p>$19/user/month (annual). Everything in Basic plus unlimited minutes (landline &amp; mobile), forward
    to device, ring all devices, AI Global Connect, 100 SMS, free omnichannel inbox, basic analytics,
    and 50+ integrations (Zapier, Slack, Webhook). Best for small teams.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Professional $30/user/month</h3>

    <p>$30/user/month (annual). Everything in Starter plus call recordings, multilingual IVR, call distribution,
    custom greetings, 500 SMS, AI voicemail transcription, lifetime call logs, role-based access, and
    CRM integrations (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho). The most popular tier.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Ultimate $45/user/month</h3>

    <p>$45/user/month (annual). Everything in Professional plus smart DID routing, multilevel IVR, call
    blocking, holiday and CRM-based VIP routing, 1,000 SMS, SSO, custom integrations, dedicated account
    manager, personalized onboarding, and 24×7 phone support. Built for advanced operations.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Enterprise &amp; Add-ons</h3>

    <p>Enterprise is custom-quoted (minimum 50 users) with SLA, custom reports, and CallHippo AI included.
    Optional add-ons include Call Scribe and Dashboard User ($15/user/month each), CNAM setup ($100 one-time),
    Spam Watch ($3/number/month), and an AI coaching add-on (~$100/user/month).</p>
