    <p>RescueTime offers two Solo (individual) plans and two Team plans, all billed monthly or annually.
    Annual billing is cheaper per month, and a 14-day free trial of the paid features lets you test before
    committing.</p>

    <h3>Solo &mdash; $7/month (or $84/year)</h3>

    <p>For individuals who want automatic time tracking, detailed reports, focus sessions with distraction
    blocking, daily highlights, goals, alerts, and unlimited history. Billed annually at $84 it works
    out to $7/month.</p>

    <h3>Solo+ &mdash; $12/month (or $144/year)</h3>

    <p>Everything in Solo plus advanced features and integrations for power users who want deeper analytics
    and more control over their productivity data.</p>

    <h3>Team &mdash; $10/month per user (or $120/year per user)</h3>

    <p>Team dashboards, account management, and per-seat pricing for organizations that want shared productivity
    insights across the team.</p>

    <h3>Team+ &mdash; $16/month per user (or $192/year per user)</h3>

    <p>Everything in Team plus enhanced administration, reporting, and management features for larger
    or more demanding organizations.</p>
