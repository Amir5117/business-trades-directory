    <p>$34.99/user/month ($419.99 billed annually). Adds custom domains, advanced analytics, lead-capture
    forms, integrations, and priority support. Recommended for marketing and sales teams.</p>

    <p>Custom pricing — contact sales. Includes SSO, dedicated success manager, custom integrations, advanced
