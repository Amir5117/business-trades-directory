    <h3>Cons</h3>

    <ul>

    <li>❌ Higher-tier Pro and Ultimate suites can feel expensive compared to free rivals</li>

    <li>❌ Frequent upgrade reminders and upsell pop-ups can annoy some users</li>

    <li>❌ No permanently free version—only a time-limited 21-day trial</li>

    <h2>What real WinZip customers say</h2>

    <p>WinZip is sold as one-time-purchase suites (Standard, Pro and Ultimate) as well as an annual subscription,
    so most reviewers frame it as a paid alternative to free tools like 7-Zip and the built-in Windows
    zip feature. Across Capterra, TrustRadius and G2, long-time users consistently praise its polished
    interface, reliable AES-256 encryption, cloud and backup integrations, and the convenience of right-click
    zip, share and PDF tools. The most common criticisms are that the core compression can be matched
    by free utilities, that upsell prompts and the renewal price feel aggressive, and that the suite bundles
    features many casual users never touch. For business users who want a supported, easy-to-use file
    manager with encryption and sharing built in, WinZip still earns solid ratings; bargain hunters often
    weigh it against free options first.</p>'
