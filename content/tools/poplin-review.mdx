    <h2>Poplin Pricing Plans</h2>

    <p>Poplin uses simple per-pound laundry pricing with free pickup and delivery, rather than fixed subscription
    tiers. The rates below are taken directly from the official Poplin pricing and FAQ pages.</p>

    <p><strong>Standard (Next-Day) Service &mdash; $1/lb</strong></p>

    <p>Next-day wash, dry, and fold at just $1 per pound in most zip codes (slightly higher in some areas).
    Free pickup and delivery. Best for everyday household laundry on a flexible timeline.</p>

    <p><strong>Express (Same-Day / Overnight) Service &mdash; $2/lb</strong></p>

    <p>Same-day or overnight turnaround at $2 per pound in most zip codes. Free pickup and delivery included.
    Best when you need your laundry back fast.</p>

    <p><strong>Oversized Items &mdash; +$8 per item</strong></p>

    <p>Bulky items like comforters that need their own wash/dry cycle are an extra $8 each, added on top
    of the per-pound rate.</p>

    <p><strong>Minimum Order &mdash; $30</strong></p>

    <p>The minimum charge per order is $30. If your weight times the per-pound rate is less than $30,
    you are charged the $30 minimum. There is no per-bag fee &mdash; you pay only by weight (measured
    after drying).</p>

    <p>Note: Pricing is per pound with free pickup and delivery; rates may be higher in certain zip codes,
    and occasional surge pricing or order-protection fees are shown clearly before you confirm an order.</p>
