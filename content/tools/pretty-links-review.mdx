  overview_listing:
  - overview_listing_title: Branded short URL cloaking
    overview_listing_content: Pretty Links turns an ugly affiliate or tracking URL into a branded short
      link that lives on your own WordPress domain — for example yoursite.com/go/shopname. Branded cloaking
      builds trust and keeps long, parameter-heavy URLs out of your posts.
  - overview_listing_content: Every Pretty Link ships with built-in click analytics — total clicks, unique
      clicks, referrer, country, device and a time-series graph. You can track which blog post or email
      drives the most affiliate clicks without bolting on a third-party tracker.
    overview_listing_title: Click tracking with detailed analytics
  - overview_listing_title: Auto-linking of keywords
    overview_listing_content: Auto-linking scans your content for chosen keywords and rewrites them to
      a Pretty Link across your entire site. Mention 'Bluehost' in a post and every instance is replaced
      with your affiliate link automatically, retroactive on old posts too.
  - overview_listing_content: The Pro importer accepts a CSV of long URLs and target slugs and creates
      hundreds of Pretty Links in one run. Bulk import is how agencies or serious affiliates migrate from
      Bit.ly or a manual .htaccess system in minutes instead of days.
    overview_listing_title: Bulk redirect import
  - overview_listing_title: Split testing for URLs
    overview_listing_content: Split testing lets one Pretty Link rotate between two or more destination
      URLs, either evenly or weighted, and reports which variant converts best. It is the simplest way
      to a/b test a sales page or affiliate offer without Optimizely.
  - overview_listing_title: Geographic & device targeting
    overview_listing_content: Targeting rules can route clicks to a different URL based on visitor country,
      device (mobile/desktop), or referring domain. For example, send US visitors to Amazon US and EU
      visitors to Amazon DE from the same link in a review post.
  - overview_listing_content: Pretty Links Amazon Autolinker localises Amazon product links to the visitor's
      Amazon storefront automatically, helping international traffic earn commissions where Amazon operates.
      Bloggers recover revenue that would otherwise be lost on foreign clicks.
    overview_listing_title: Amazon product link automation
  - overview_listing_title: Nofollow / noindex controls
    overview_listing_content: Per-link nofollow, noindex and sponsored attributes are toggled inside Pretty
      Links rather than typed into each post. Affiliate marketers comply with Google's sponsored-link
      guidelines across thousands of outbound links with one setting per link.
