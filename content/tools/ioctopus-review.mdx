In this IOctopus review by [TheBusinessTrades](https://thebusinesstrades.com/).com, we look at its pricing, features, integrations, and ease of use based on public reviews and user feedback. Whether you run paid ads on Google, Meta, TikTok, or Pinterest, IOctopus promises reliable conversion tracking, server\-side tagging, and improved ROAS visibility.

Our analysis of IOctopus shows the platform is trusted for bypassing ad blockers, recovering attribution lost to iOS 14\.5\+ ATT restrictions, and centralizing server\-side tracking across multiple ad platforms. It holds a 4\.7/5 rating across user reviews. Users praise the no\-code setup, GTM compatibility, and pixel matching accuracy, while some flag the learning curve for advanced server containers. For DTC brands and agencies serious about attribution, IOctopus is a strong choice.
