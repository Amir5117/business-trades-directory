  review_content: '<p>Xero offers four straightforward US pricing plans, with optional add-ons for expenses
    and project tracking. All paid plans include core accounting features and unlimited users:</p>

    <p><strong>Lite</strong>: $7/month — simple bookkeeping for micro-businesses and side hustles. Send
    5 invoices and quotes, accept online invoice payments, reconcile bank transactions, capture bills
    and receipts with Hubdoc and view real-time reports.<br />

    <strong>Starter</strong>: $29/month — good for sole traders, new businesses and the self-employed.
    Send quotes and 20 invoices, accept online invoice payments, enter 5 bills, reconcile bank transactions,
    Hubdoc capture, real-time reports and a 30-day cash flow forecast.<br />

    <strong>Standard</strong>: $50/month — accounting tools for the self-employed and growing businesses.
    Everything in Starter plus unlimited invoices and bills, auto-reconcile (Beta), bulk reconcile, 60-day
    cash flow forecast, performance graphs, customisable dashboards and KPI analysis.<br />

    <strong>Premium</strong>: $75/month — good for established businesses of all sizes. Everything in
    Standard plus multi-currency support, 180-day cash flow forecast, tailored financial health scorecards
    and industry benchmarking.</p>

    <p>Optional add-ons available across paid plans: <strong>Claim expenses</strong> from $4/month and
    <strong>Track projects</strong> from $7/month. A 30-day free trial is available, and Xero is currently
    offering 80% off your plan for the first 3 months. Pricing varies by region — UK, Australia, NZ, Canada
    and EU markets use local currencies and slightly different feature mixes and plan names.</p>

    <h2>Pros and Cons of Xero</h2>

    <h3>Pros</h3>

    <ul>

    <li>✅ Clean, modern UI that is easy for non-accountants</li>

    <li>✅ Daily bank feeds and powerful reconciliation rules</li>

    <li>✅ MTD VAT, RTI payroll and HMRC compliance built in</li>

    <li>✅ 1,000+ app integrations across the small-business stack</li>

    <li>✅ Unlimited users on every plan with role-based access</li>

    </ul>

    <h3>Cons</h3>

    <ul>

    <li>❌ Lower plans cap the number of invoices and bills</li>

    <li>❌ Payroll add-ons cost extra in some regions</li>

    <li>❌ Phone support is limited; help is mostly by email</li>

    <li>❌ Reporting customisation is less flexible than enterprise tools</li>

    </ul>
