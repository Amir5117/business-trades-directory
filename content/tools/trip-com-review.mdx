    <p>Trip.com doesn''t sell subscriptions — it''s a free-to-use travel booking platform that earns commissions

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Booking Platform — Free to Use</h3>

    <p>$0/month. Browsing, searching, comparing, and booking flights, hotels, trains, cars, and attractions
    on Trip.com is free. You only pay for the underlying travel product you book, plus any service fees
    disclosed at checkout.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Trip.com Rewards — Free Membership</h3>

    <p>$0 to join. Trip.com Rewards is the loyalty program: guests get basic rewards, while free registered
    members unlock extra Trip Coins and additional travel perks. Trip Coins are earned on eligible bookings
    and redeemed against future bookings.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Trip Coins Loyalty — Earn As You Book</h3>

    <p>Earn Trip Coins on most eligible bookings and redeem them for discounts on future bookings. Members
    receive more exclusive rewards and travel perks than non-members, with the value depending on what
    and how often you book.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Trip.com Credit Card / Trip Plus (Select Markets)</h3>

    <p>Custom pricing — varies by region. In select markets Trip.com partners with banks on co-branded
    credit cards (elevated rewards, travel insurance, lounge access) and offers optional paid memberships
    such as Trip Plus with instant hotel discounts and premium support.</p>


    <h2>Pros and Cons of Trip.com</h2>

    <ul>

    <li>✅ 24/7 multi-language customer support in 19+ languages.</li>

    <li>✅ Trip Coins genuinely accumulate to meaningful discounts for frequent travellers.</li>

    <li>✅ Free cancellation available on many hotel bookings.</li>

    <li>✅ Price-match guarantee on qualifying bookings.</li>

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Cons</h3>
