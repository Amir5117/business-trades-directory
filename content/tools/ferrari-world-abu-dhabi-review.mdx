    World Abu Dhabi is the world's first Ferrari-branded theme park, located on Yas Island and easily

    m² of indoor entertainment, Ferrari World combines high-speed thrills with Italian heritage and the
    prancing horse legacy.</p><p>The park is famous for Formula Rossa, the fastest roller coaster on the
    planet, which launches riders from 0 to 240 km/h (149 mph) in just 4.9 seconds. Other signature rides
    include Flying Aces (the world's tallest roller coaster loop), Mission Ferrari (a multi-sensory family
    coaster), Turbo Track (vertical drop and launch), Driving Experience (Maranello-quality simulators),
    and Karting Academy for younger guests. The park is air-conditioned year-round, making it the perfect
    attraction during Abu Dhabi's hot summers. Tickets can be bundled with Yas Waterworld, Warner Bros.
    World, and SeaWorld Abu Dhabi for multi-park experiences.</p>
