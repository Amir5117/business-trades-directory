    overview_listing_content: Hotjar's signature heatmaps visualize every click, tap, and scroll on your
      key pages, so you can instantly see what visitors engage with — and what they ignore. Click, move,
      scroll, and engagement-zone variants help PMs and designers prioritize layout fixes with real data
      instead of opinions.
  - overview_listing_content: Hotjar Session Recordings replay real visitor sessions frame-by-frame, capturing
      mouse movement, clicks, taps, and form interactions. Filter by URL, device, traffic source, or rage-click
      frequency to jump straight to the friction points without watching every recording.
    overview_listing_title: Session Recordings
  - overview_listing_title: Surveys and NPS
    overview_listing_content: Hotjar Surveys let you ask on-page or post-purchase questions with logic
      branching, multi-choice, and open-text fields, plus a built-in NPS template. Responses are linked
      back to the user's recording, so qualitative answers always have the matching session context attached.
  - overview_listing_content: Incoming Feedback widgets sit unobtrusively on your site and let visitors
      react to specific page elements with a thumbs up/down plus comment, even highlighting the exact
      element they're commenting on. It surfaces problems your funnel reports won't catch — copy confusion,
      broken images, dark patterns.
    overview_listing_title: Incoming Feedback
  - overview_listing_title: Funnels and Conversion Analysis
    overview_listing_content: Funnels show drop-off rates between steps in any user journey and link directly
      to recordings of users who abandoned each step. You stop guessing why conversion is leaking and
      watch the actual moment users left.
  - overview_listing_title: Engagement Zones
    overview_listing_content: Engagement Zones combine click, move, and scroll heatmaps into a single
      visual layer that scores every section of a page from cold to hot. It's the fastest way to find
      which fold or block is dragging the rest of the page down without staring at three separate heatmaps.
  - overview_listing_content: Hotjar lets you target heatmaps, recordings, and surveys by URL, device,
      traffic source, scroll depth, time on page, user attributes, and custom events on Business+ plans.
      PII suppression, key-stroke masking, GDPR/CCPA controls, and EU data residency keep legal and security
      teams happy.

      team already lives in. The Trends and Highlights dashboards roll qualitative + quantitative signals
      into shareable views for product reviews and stakeholder readouts.
