    <ul>

    <li>✅ 99.999% uptime SLA with 8 globally distributed data centers</li>

    <li>✅ U.S.-based 24/7 "Amazing Service" support included on all plans</li>

    <li>✅ Unified NextivaONE app for voice, video, chat, SMS</li>

    <li>✅ Generous unlimited US/Canada calling in every paid plan</li>

    <li>✅ Native HubSpot, Salesforce, Zendesk, Outlook integrations</li>

    <li>✅ Free number porting from your existing provider</li>

    </ul>

    <h3>Cons</h3>

    <ul>

    <li>❌ Per-user pricing scales steeply for large teams vs. flat-rate competitors</li>

    <li>❌ Some advanced AI features locked behind Power Suite tier</li>

    <li>❌ Initial onboarding can take 1–2 weeks for complex IVR builds</li>

    <li>❌ Annual billing required for the published rates (monthly is ~30% more)</li>

    </ul>
