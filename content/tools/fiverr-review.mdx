  services_section_title: Fiverr Pricing Plans
  services_section_content: '<p>Fiverr is a freelance services marketplace — there is no monthly platform
    subscription for buyers. Service prices ("gigs") are set by individual sellers, typically ranging
    from $5 to several thousand dollars. Fiverr charges a service fee to buyers and a 20% commission to
    sellers.</p>

    <ul>

    <li><strong>Buyer Pricing:</strong> Gig prices range from $5 (Basic) to $1,000+ (Premium) — most freelance
    services fall in the $25-$500 range</li>

    <li><strong>Buyer Service Fee:</strong> 5.5% added to all orders, plus a $2.50 small-order fee on
    orders under $50</li>

    <li><strong>Seller Commission:</strong> Fiverr takes 20% of every order (including tips and gig extras);
    sellers receive 80% of the gig price</li>

    <li><strong>Fiverr Pro:</strong> Vetted top-tier talent — Fiverr Pro Essential is included free with
    eligibility; Fiverr Pro Advanced is $129/month for premium business features</li>

    <li><strong>Seller Plus:</strong> $39/month — analytics, dedicated success manager, advanced promotion
    tools for established sellers</li>

    <li><strong>Fiverr Business:</strong> Free for buyers — team workspaces, dedicated business success
    manager, vetted Pro talent, project management tools</li>

    </ul>

    <p>Payment to sellers is held in escrow during delivery; sellers can withdraw via PayPal, Payoneer,
    bank transfer, or direct deposit (US) after a 14-day clearance period. Fiverr accepts all major credit
    cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay from buyers worldwide.</p>'
