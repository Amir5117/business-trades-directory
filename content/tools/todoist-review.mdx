  review_content: '<h2>Todoist Pricing — What Changed After The December 2025 Raise?</h2>

    Todoist has historically been one of the most affordable productivity apps on the market, and even
    after its <strong>December 2025 price increase</strong> (Pro monthly up ~40%, annual up ~25%), it
    remains dramatically cheaper than Notion, ClickUp, or Asana for the solo workflow. Todoist uses a
    three-tier pricing structure — Free, Pro, and Business.


    The <strong>Free plan ($0/month)</strong> ships 5 active projects, 5 collaborators per project, 3
    filter views, Smart Quick Add with natural-language parsing, basic reminders, Ramble voice capture,
    and full cross-device sync. For light personal task management it is genuinely enough — and many long-time
    Todoist users stay on Free for years.


    The <strong>Pro plan now lands around $5/month billed annually (or $6/month monthly)</strong> after
    the December 2025 adjustment. It unlocks 300 active projects, 25 collaborators per project, 150 filter
    views, unlimited labels, calendar layout, reminders (time and location), AI assistant, themes, 1GB
    file uploads, and priority support. For any solo knowledge worker, the Pro tier pays for itself in
    capture and planning time.


    The <strong>Business plan starts at $8/user/month (annual) or $10/user/month (monthly)</strong> and
    adds team workspaces, admin controls, centralized billing, team templates, shared filters and labels,
    role-based permissions, and team activity log. For teams under 25, Todoist Business is a genuinely
    lighter-weight alternative to Asana Basic or ClickUp Unlimited at a fraction of the price.


    Compared to <strong>Things 3</strong> (one-time $49.99 on each platform but no sync outside Apple
    ecosystem), <strong>TickTick</strong> ($3.99/month but weaker natural-language parsing and less polished
    mobile), and <strong>Notion</strong> ($10/month but not a dedicated task manager), Todoist Pro remains
    one of the best value-per-dollar productivity subscriptions. The free tier gives you 30 days to validate
    the workflow before any upgrade, and Doist (the makers of Todoist) have never had a data breach or
    ownership change in 15+ years of operation.'
