    and annual. Prices can vary by region and promotional offer, and a 7-day free trial is included with
    all paid plans. The free tier gives access to one book summary per day, while Premium unlocks the
    full library of 1,500+ summaries, audio versions, gamification, and offline downloads.


    Free plan: $0 - one book summary per day, basic personalization, limited features.

    Monthly Premium: $12.99/month - full access to 1,500+ summaries, audio playback, daily insights, mind
    maps, offline access, multiple device sync.

    Quarterly Premium: $29.99 for 3 months (about $10/month) - same features as Monthly with mid-term
    commitment savings.

    Annual Premium: $89.99/year (about $7.50/month) - same Premium features, best price for committed
    users.

    Lifetime Premium: occasionally $299 one-time (promotional) - one-time payment for permanent Premium
    access; promo deals frequently drop this to $39.99-$79.99 on StackSocial and similar outlets.


    All Premium plans include access on iOS, Android, and the Headway web app. Promotional discounts,
    student offers, and lifetime deals are released throughout the year. Subscriptions auto-renew at standard
    rates unless cancelled in App Store, Google Play, or Headway account settings before the renewal date.'
