    <li><strong>Essential:</strong> from $87/month — up to 11 data sources, 1 user, daily data refreshes,
    basic support. Best for solopreneurs and small marketing teams using a single destination.</li>

    <li><strong>Core:</strong> from $299/month — up to 30 data sources, multiple users, hourly refreshes,
    advanced transformation, and priority support. Built for in-house marketing teams and small agencies.</li>

    <li><strong>Super:</strong> from $724/month — up to 82 data sources, unlimited users, near-real-time
    refreshes, custom field combinations, and account management. Designed for mid-market and enterprise
    teams.</li>

    <li><strong>Enterprise:</strong> Custom pricing — dedicated infrastructure, SSO, audit logs, custom
    SLAs, and dedicated customer success. For large agencies and global brands.</li>

    </ul>

    <p>For single-destination connectors (e.g., Supermetrics for Google Sheets or Looker Studio), entry
    pricing starts lower — around $29–$39/month for limited connectors. Each destination requires its
    own subscription, so total cost depends on your stack. A 14-day free trial is available, and annual
    billing offers around 20% savings.</p>'
