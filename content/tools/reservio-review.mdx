  - overview_listing_title: Online payments via Stripe
    overview_listing_content: Take card payments and deposits at booking through Stripe, so clients pre-pay

  - overview_listing_content: Recurring appointment rules let a yoga studio, music teacher or wellness

      they need. You can mark fields as required or conditional per service.
