  review_content: '<h2>Lusha Pricing — Is The Pro Plan At $69/User Worth It?</h2>

    Lusha uses a <strong>credit-based subscription model</strong> in 2026 with four tiers — Free, Pro,
    Premium, and Scale — and each credit unlocks one verified email or one direct-dial phone number. That
    means credit-economics matter more than the headline price, and Lusha deliberately sizes each tier
    around a realistic outbound workflow.


    The <strong>Free plan ($0/month)</strong> ships 5 credits per month — enough to test accuracy but
    not enough for real outbound cadence. Many founders and job seekers use this tier indefinitely for
    one-off prospecting.


    The <strong>Pro plan starts at $69/user/month (annual)</strong> and includes 960 credits/year (approximately
    80/month), the Chrome extension with full LinkedIn and Sales Navigator support, basic CRM integrations
    (HubSpot, Salesforce), and bulk enrichment. For small SDR teams and founder-led outbound, this is
    the sweet spot.


    The <strong>Premium plan at $99/user/month</strong> adds 5,400 credits/year, advanced prospecting
    filters, intent signals, job change alerts, and deeper CRM sync (Pipedrive, Zoho, Outreach, Salesloft).
    Teams doing serious daily prospecting land here.


    The <strong>Scale plan is custom-priced</strong> and unlocks API access, SSO, SCIM provisioning, team
    analytics, dedicated CSM, and the Lusha AI intent and fit-scoring layer — typically starting around
    $1,500+/month for a 5-seat enterprise package.


    Compared to ZoomInfo ($15,000+ annual minimum), Lusha''s per-seat pricing is dramatically more accessible
    for small and mid-market teams. Compared to Apollo.io, Lusha is more expensive per credit but historically
    delivers higher direct-dial accuracy — especially on senior B2B contacts in the US and Western Europe.
    For most small teams running outbound on LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Pro or Premium is the right starting
    point, and the free tier lets you validate match rates against your exact ICP before committing.'
