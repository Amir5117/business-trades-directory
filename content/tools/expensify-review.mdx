  review_content: '<h2>Expensify Price Plans</h2>

    <p>Expensify uses per-member pricing, with the Expensify Card lowering the effective rate when a share
    of company spend runs on the card. Expensify has streamlined its lineup to two paid plans plus a free
    option for individuals. Prices below are from expensify.com/pricing (USD per member per month).</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Collect — $5/member/month</h3>

    <p>$5/member/month. Adds corporate card programme, custom approval workflows, multi-level admin, and
    tag-based reporting. Standard mid-market tier.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Control — from $9/active member/month</h3>

    <p>Custom pricing, as low as $9 per active member/month. Adds advanced policy compliance, NetSuite/Sage
    Intacct sync, SAML SSO, per-diem rules, and dedicated support. Built for finance teams scaling globally.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Free for Individuals</h3>

    <p>$0/month. SmartScan receipt capture limited to 25 scans/month for individual users — useful for
    personal tracking before upgrading.</p>

    <h2>Pros and Cons of Expensify</h2>

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Pros</h3>

    <ul>

    <li>✅ SmartScan OCR is genuinely fast and accurate, even on poor-quality receipts.</li>

    <li>✅ Expensify Card eliminates end-of-month receipt chasing.</li>

    <li>✅ Native integrations cover virtually every major accounting platform.</li>

    <li>✅ Mobile app is excellent — receipt capture in seconds.</li>

    <li>✅ Multi-currency, multi-language, and global reimbursements work out of the box.</li>

    <li>✅ Half-price pricing on Collect and Control tiers when 50%+ of spend uses the Expensify Card.</li>

    </ul>

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Cons</h3>

    <ul>

    <li>❌ Pricing rises sharply if you don''t use the Expensify Card.</li>

    <li>❌ The dual product experience (classic Expensify vs New Expensify) can be confusing.</li>

    <li>❌ Customer support response times can be slow on lower tiers.</li>

    <li>❌ Complex approval policies require admin setup time to configure correctly.</li>

    </ul>
