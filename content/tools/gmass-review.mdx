  review_content: '<h2>GMass Price Plans</h2>

    <p>GMass offers a free tier plus paid plans for individuals and team pricing, all run inside Gmail.
    Because GMass updates its plan pricing periodically, please check the official gmass.co/pricing page
    for the current monthly and annual rates in your region.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Free</h3>

    <p>Up to 50 emails per day, basic mail merge, and limited tracking — enough to evaluate the workflow
    inside Gmail.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Standard</h3>

    <p>Unlimited sends (subject to Gmail''s own daily limits), basic auto follow-ups, basic reports, and
    Gmail support. Best for individual users testing outbound.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Premium</h3>

    <p>Adds advanced behaviour-based campaigns, sequence pausing, reply detection, and detailed analytics.
    Recommended tier for active cold-email senders and recruiters.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Professional</h3>

    <p>Adds priority support, custom tracking domains, A/B testing, premium deliverability tools, and
    unlimited sequences — built for high-volume professional senders.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Team Plans</h3>

    <p>Custom pricing — contact sales. Volume discounts, shared templates, central billing, and team-level
    reporting for agencies and sales teams.</p>


    <h2>Pros and Cons of GMass</h2>

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Pros</h3>

    <ul>

    <li>✅ Lives inside Gmail — no need to learn a new platform.</li>

    <li>✅ Uses your own Gmail/Workspace account for sending, giving strong native deliverability.</li>

    <li>✅ Powerful Google Sheets integration for dynamic lists.</li>

    <li>✅ Automatic follow-ups with smart pause-on-reply behaviour.</li>

    <li>✅ Free tier and a 14-day full-feature trial make it easy to test.</li>

    <li>✅ Built-in inbox placement test and deliverability tooling.</li>

    </ul>

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Cons</h3>

    <ul>

    <li>❌ Daily send limits are still bound by Gmail/Google Workspace caps.</li>

    <li>❌ Reporting UI is functional but less polished than dedicated SaaS competitors.</li>

    <li>❌ No native CRM — you need a separate tool for full pipeline tracking.</li>

    <li>❌ Advanced personalisation can require some spreadsheet skill to set up.</li>

    </ul>
