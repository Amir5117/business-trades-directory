    <p>Monument Grills is headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, with manufacturing partners overseas. The

    <h3>Is Monument Grills better than Weber?</h3>

    <p>Monument Grills offer many comparable features (Clearview lid, LED knobs, side sear burner, multi-burner
    setups) at a significantly lower price than Weber. Weber still leads in long-term build quality and
    resale value, but Monument is widely considered the better value pick.</p>


    <h3>Do Monument Grills come with a warranty?</h3>

    <p>Yes. Standard Monument gas grills come with a multi-year limited warranty, and the premium Eminence
    405/605 series includes a 10-year limited warranty.</p>


    <h3>Where can I buy Monument Grills?</h3>

    <p>You can buy directly from monumentgrills.com or through major retailers like Amazon, Home Depot,
    Lowe''s, and Walmart. Buying direct typically gives you the best access to support and replacement
    parts.</p>


    <h3>Are Monument Grills good for smoking?</h3>

    <p>Their pellet grills are designed for low-and-slow smoking, while their gas models can hold steady
    low temperatures with a smoker box accessory. Dedicated pellet smokers from Traeger or Camp Chef may
    give you more advanced smoke profiles.</p>


    <h3>Do Monument Grills have WiFi?</h3>

    <p>Select premium Monument pellet and gas models now include Bluetooth or WiFi connectivity for app-based
    monitoring and recipe control.</p>
