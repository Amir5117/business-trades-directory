Signable is a UK e\-signature platform that makes sending, signing, and tracking legally binding documents fast and affordable. In this Signable review by [TheBusinessTrades](https://thebusinesstrades.com/).com, we look at its pricing, features, compliance, and ease of use based on public Trustpilot, Capterra, and G2 reviews. Whether you are a UK\-based business needing GDPR compliance or a global team wanting a DocuSign alternative, Signable offers e\-signatures, templates, audit trails, and API access at a competitive price.

Our analysis of Signable [e\-signature reviews](https://thebusinesstrades.com/category/e-signature-tools/) shows the platform is trusted for UK data residency, simplicity, and customer support. It holds a 4\.7/5 rating on Trustpilot across 250\+ reviews. Users praise the unlimited templates, friendly UK support, and Eolas compliance, while some flag the basic mobile app. For UK businesses wanting a homegrown e\-signature solution, Signable is a top choice.
