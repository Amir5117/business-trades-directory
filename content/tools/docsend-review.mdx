  faqs_section:
  - faqs_area_title: Is DocSend secure for confidential documents?
    faqs_area_content: Yes. DocSend supports email verification, passcodes, NDA gating, link expiry, dynamic
      watermarking, and access revocation. The platform is SOC 2 Type II compliant and inherits Dropbox's
      enterprise security infrastructure.
  - faqs_area_content: For most fundraising and partnership use cases, yes — the Advanced plan and Advanced
      Data Rooms tier offer folder structures, bulk permissions, dynamic watermarking, and audit logs.
      For large complex M&A you may still want a dedicated VDR like Datasite.
    faqs_area_title: Can DocSend replace a full virtual data room?
  - faqs_area_title: How much does DocSend cost?
    faqs_area_content: Plans start at $15/user/month (Personal), $65/user/month (Standard), and $250/user/month
      (Advanced, 3-user minimum). Custom enterprise pricing is available for Advanced Data Rooms.
  - faqs_area_title: Can DocSend track if someone forwards my link?
    faqs_area_content: Yes. Email verification gates link access per recipient, so if your link is forwarded,
      the new viewer must verify their own email — and you will see them as a separate visitor in your
      analytics.
  - faqs_area_content: Yes, eSignature is included in the Standard plan and above. You can request legally
      binding signatures on documents shared through DocSend without using a separate signing platform.
    faqs_area_title: Does DocSend offer eSignature?
  - faqs_area_title: How is DocSend different from Dropbox itself?
    faqs_area_content: DocSend is owned by Dropbox but focuses specifically on tracked, secure, branded
      external sharing with analytics. Regular Dropbox is built for storage and collaboration rather than
      per-page engagement tracking and recipient-level access controls.
