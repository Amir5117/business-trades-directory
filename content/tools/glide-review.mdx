  review_content: '<h2>Glide Price Plans</h2>

    <p>Glide offers a generous free tier plus four paid plans (Explorer, Maker, Business, Enterprise).
    All paid plans are available month-to-month or annually with a meaningful annual discount. Below are
    the current public prices as of 2026.</p>

    <h3>Free Plan — $0</h3>

    <p>One published app for up to 10 personal users, 25,000 rows, Glide Tables data source, community
    support. Great for trying Glide before upgrading.</p>

    <h3>Explorer — $19/month</h3>

    <p>Unlimited apps, workflows, Glide AI, more integrations, and increased row limits. Best for solo
    makers and individual app builders.</p>

    <h3>Maker — $49/month</h3>

    <p>Designed for MVPs, prototyping, communities, and schools. Includes unlimited personal users, larger
    row limits, and additional Glide AI capacity.</p>

    <h3>Business — $199/month</h3>

    <p>Adds Airtable, Excel, Glide API, work-email sign-in, and stronger security controls. Includes 30
    users; additional users $5/month each. Best for SMBs and operations teams.</p>

    <h3>Enterprise — Custom pricing</h3>

    <p>SSO, audit logs, dedicated support, custom data residency, contractual SLAs, and bespoke implementation
    help. Quote on request from Glide sales.</p>
