  review_content: '<h2>Domo Price Plans</h2>

    <p>Domo uses a consumption-based pricing model — most plans are quote-only and tailored to the number
    of users, data refresh frequency, and required features. Below is what Domo publicly discloses on
    domo.com/pricing.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Free Trial</h3>

    <p>Free 30-day trial — no credit card required. Includes access to the Domo platform with sample data
    sets and core dashboards plus Magic ETL, so individuals and teams can evaluate the product before
    requesting a quote.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Standard</h3>

    <p>Custom pricing — contact sales. Includes core BI features, 1,000+ connectors, basic governance,
    and pay-as-you-go credits aligned to user activity rather than per-seat licenses.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Enterprise</h3>

    <p>Custom pricing — contact sales. Adds SSO, advanced governance, role-based permissions, larger data
    volumes, SLAs, and access to AI/ML capabilities (Domo.AI, AutoML, DomoGPT).</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Business Critical</h3>

    <p>Custom pricing — contact sales. Premium tier with private link, HIPAA compliance, customer-managed
    encryption keys, advanced security, and Domo Everywhere embedded analytics for customer-facing use
    cases.</p>


    <h2>Pros and Cons of Domo</h2>

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Pros</h3>

    <ul>

    <li>✅ One platform replaces ETL, BI, alerting, and embedded analytics for many organisations.</li>

    <li>✅ 1,000+ pre-built connectors make it easy to centralise scattered SaaS and database data.</li>

    <li>✅ Magic ETL gives business analysts powerful transformations without writing SQL.</li>

    <li>✅ Strong mobile experience — leadership teams can monitor KPIs from native iOS/Android apps.</li>

    <li>✅ AI service layer (Domo.AI, DomoGPT, AutoML) brings ML capabilities to business users.</li>

    <li>✅ Robust governance, lineage, and certified data sets fit enterprise compliance needs.</li>

    </ul>

    <h3 style="font-size:22px;font-weight:500;">Cons</h3>

    <ul>

    <li>❌ Pricing is opaque and can be expensive at enterprise scale, especially with high data volumes.</li>

    <li>❌ Consumption-based pricing makes annual budget forecasting tricky.</li>

    <li>❌ The platform is feature-dense; new users often need formal training to use Magic ETL effectively.</li>

    <li>❌ Some advanced data transformations are still easier in a dedicated warehouse + ETL stack.</li>

    </ul>
